About the class

Gold Coast Cooking Class is a home kitchen in East Legon, Accra. Guests come to cook Ghanaian food the way it is actually made — at the stove, with their own hands, in a house that smells of ginger, pepper, and tomatoes before the first pot even comes to a simmer.

This is not a demonstration from a stage. Classes are small and personal. You chop, stir, taste, and ask questions while the meal comes together. Locals come to cook with more confidence. Visitors come to learn the dishes they have been eating all week. Everyone leaves having cooked, eaten, and talked.

The kitchen is ordinary on purpose: a real stove, real pots, the same ingredients you would find in a Ghanaian market. What you take home is not a performance. It is a meal you made, and the knowledge to make it again.

The table is the classroom.

Featured dishes

Kelewele

Ripe plantain, cut into chunks and tossed with ginger, onion, and pepper, then fried until the edges caramelise. Kelewele is the snack that disappears first — hot, fragrant, and a little fiery.

Omu Tuo and groundnut soup

Soft rice balls — omu tuo — served with groundnut soup and goat meat. Nutty, slow-cooked, and meant to be eaten with your hands, it is one of the quiet comforts of a Ghanaian table.

Also cooked in class

When the day’s menu allows, classes also cover Jollof Rice, Fried Rice, Banku and Tilapia, Fufu and Light Soup, Waakye, and Red Red. Ask when you book — the kitchen cooks what the group is hungry to learn.

What’s included

  • Hands-on cooking in a home kitchen in East Legon, Accra.
  • The dishes you prepare, served at the table.
  • Homemade Ghanaian drinks — sobolo, mango-pineapple, pineapple-passion, and other seasonal blends — poured throughout the class.
  • Local sweets from the kitchen, offered alongside the drinks.

Class length, group size, and price are confirmed when you get in touch. There is no minimum or maximum number of guests.
